About Big Hit Games

We create mobile games with a clear focus on fun and usability.

Big Hit Games is a game studio focused on building accessible mobile experiences across action, simulation, adventure and casual gameplay styles.

01

Our Mission

Create enjoyable mobile games that are easy to understand, responsive to play and designed with the player in mind.

02

Our Approach

Combine practical game development, clean UI/UX and iterative improvement to deliver polished player experiences.

03

Our Values

Respect players, keep experiences understandable, improve quality over time and communicate clearly with our audience.

What we do

We design and develop mobile games intended for broad audiences. Our work can include concept development, gameplay programming, user interface design, optimization, publishing preparation and post-release updates.

Player experience

We believe good mobile games should be simple to navigate, easy to start and rewarding to continue playing. We pay attention to controls, level flow, performance, readability and the overall feel of the game.

Support and transparency

If you have a question about one of our games, privacy, account-related matters or general support, you can contact us through the website contact page.
