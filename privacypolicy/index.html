Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Big Hit Games (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when you visit bighitgames.site or use mobile games published by Big Hit Games.

1. Website information

This website is primarily informational. We do not require you to create an account to browse the site. If you choose to contact us by email, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address and message content so that we can respond to your inquiry.

2. Mobile games and third-party services

Some Big Hit Games mobile applications may use third-party services for features such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, app distribution or platform functionality. Depending on the specific app and the services enabled in it, those third parties may process device or usage information under their own privacy policies.

Because individual games may use different services, users should also review any privacy disclosures shown in the relevant app, store listing or consent screen.

3. Advertising and analytics

Where a game includes advertising or analytics, the relevant providers may use identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from network data, app interaction data or diagnostic information as permitted by applicable law and platform rules. Consent choices may be presented where required.

4. Children’s privacy

Our games may have different intended audiences depending on the title. We do not knowingly use this website to collect personal information from children. For apps directed to or likely to be used by children, we aim to follow applicable platform and legal requirements for data handling and advertising.

5. Data retention

Information sent directly to us, such as support emails, may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, resolve disputes or comply with legal obligations.

6. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information under our control. However, no online system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to personal data, such as requesting access, correction or deletion. You may contact us using the details on our Contact page for privacy-related requests.

8. External links

Our website or games may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

10. Contact us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page or email us at contact@bighitgames.site.
