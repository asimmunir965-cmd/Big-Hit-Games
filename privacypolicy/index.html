Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains the general approach Big Hit Games takes to privacy for this website and our mobile games.

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Big Hit Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, and handled when you visit this website or use games published by Big Hit Games.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the game or service, we may receive limited technical and usage information such as device type, operating system, app version, gameplay events, crash data, approximate region, advertising identifiers, and interaction with advertisements or in-app features.

If you contact us directly, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, and message.

2. How Information May Be Used

  • To operate, maintain, improve, and troubleshoot our games and website.
  • To understand general usage and performance.
  • To provide support and respond to inquiries.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, or technical problems.
  • To support advertising, analytics, consent, and app functionality where applicable.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Some of our mobile games may use third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or consent-management services. These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies and applicable law. Depending on your region, consent choices may be presented before certain advertising or measurement technologies are used.

4. Children's Privacy

Our games are intended to be distributed according to the age rating and audience settings shown on their respective app-store listings. Where a product is directed to children or includes children in its intended audience, we aim to configure data practices and third-party services in line with applicable platform rules and legal requirements.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to technical, contractual, and legal requirements.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or consent withdrawal. You may contact us to ask a privacy-related question or make a request.

8. Third-Party Links and Services

Our website or games may contain links to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at contact@bighitgames.site.

Important:

This is a general website and game-publisher privacy policy template. Before publishing a specific game, update this page so it accurately reflects the SDKs, data collection, age audience, advertising setup, and legal obligations for that game.
